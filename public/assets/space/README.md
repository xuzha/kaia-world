# Space imagery

These images are bundled locally. The app does not contact the source sites at runtime.

## Milky Way

- File: `milky-way.jpg`
- Image: [The Milky Way panorama, eso0932a](https://www.eso.org/public/images/eso0932a/)
- Credit: **ESO/S. Brunier**
- Source: https://cdn.eso.org/images/large/eso0932a.jpg
- License: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- [ESO usage terms](https://www.eso.org/public/outreach/copyright/)
- Adaptation: resized from 6000 × 3000 to 4096 × 2048 and JPEG compressed;
  the renderer rotates and lightly tints the panorama to compose the scene.
- The full credit is visible in the space scene and included in exported photos.
